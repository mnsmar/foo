RNAkinet Published in NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics

September 29, 2024 · Maragkakis Lab, NIA/NIH

We are happy to share that our paper Deep learning and direct sequencing of labeled RNA captures transcriptome dynamics has been published in NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics.

RNAkinet is a neural network that identifies newly synthesized 5EU-labeled RNA directly from nanopore electrical signals, enabling simultaneous profiling of RNA metabolism, poly(A) tail length, and RNA modifications at single-molecule resolution.

Congratulations to lead author Vlastimil Martinek and all co-authors!
