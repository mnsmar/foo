Cell-specific RNA isoform remodeling in the aging mouse brain

June 8, 2025 · Maragkakis Lab, NIA/NIH

Abstract#

This study combines single-cell analysis with long-read nanopore sequencing to capture full-length RNA isoforms and uncover temporal changes in RNA transcription, processing, and alternative splicing in the aging mouse cortex and hippocampus.
