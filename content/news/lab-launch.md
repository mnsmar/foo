The Maragkakis Lab officially launched within the
[Laboratory of Genetics and Genomics](https://www.nia.nih.gov/research/labs/lgg)
at the National Institute on Aging (NIA), NIH.

The unit, led by [Dr. Emmanouil "Manolis" Maragkakis](/people/pi), investigates the
high-dimensional regulation of gene expression to uncover the mechanistic basis of
aging physiology and disease, with a focus on RNA metabolism, long-read sequencing
technologies, and machine learning.

We are actively recruiting — see our [Join Us](/join) page for open positions.
