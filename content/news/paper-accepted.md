We are happy to share that our paper
[Deep learning and direct sequencing of labeled RNA captures transcriptome dynamics](/publications/2024-rnakinet/)
has been published in **NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics**.

RNAkinet is a neural network that identifies newly synthesized 5EU-labeled RNA directly
from nanopore electrical signals, enabling simultaneous profiling of RNA metabolism,
poly(A) tail length, and RNA modifications at single-molecule resolution.

Congratulations to lead author Vlastimil Martinek and all co-authors!
