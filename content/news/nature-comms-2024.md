Our paper
[Gene body DNA hydroxymethylation restricts the magnitude of transcriptional changes during aging](/publications/2024-dna-hydroxymethylation-aging/)
is now published in **Nature Communications**.

This work reveals that gene body 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (5hmC) acts as an epigenetic
brake on transcriptional dysregulation during aging — a novel mechanism restraining the
magnitude of age-associated gene expression changes.

Congratulations to all co-authors!
