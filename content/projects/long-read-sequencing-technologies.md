## Overview

Existing short-read sequencing approaches cannot capture the full complexity of the
transcriptome, including RNA isoforms, poly(A) tail lengths, RNA modifications, and
RNA stability dynamics simultaneously. We develop technologies based on nanopore
direct RNA sequencing — which reads full-length native RNA molecules — to overcome
these limitations.

## Approach

We build experimental workflows for metabolic labeling of nascent RNA and single-cell
long-read sequencing, combined with computational tools and machine learning models
that extract multi-dimensional RNA features from raw nanopore signal.

## Key Tools

**RNAkinet** — A deep learning framework that identifies newly synthesized 5EU-labeled
RNA directly from nanopore electrical signals, enabling simultaneous profiling of RNA
metabolism, poly(A) tail length, and RNA modifications at single-molecule resolution
without basecalling.
