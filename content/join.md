The Maragkakis Lab is interdisciplinary, combining experimental and computational biology.
Ideal candidates have a passion for understanding how RNA and gene regulation drive aging,
and enthusiasm for developing and applying cutting-edge sequencing technologies and
machine learning tools.

## Postdoctoral Fellows

The lab has openings for postdoctoral fellows with expertise in one or more of:

- RNA biology, transcriptomics, or epitranscriptomics
- Nanopore / long-read sequencing
- Computational genomics or bioinformatics
- Machine learning applied to biological data
- Aging, senescence, or neurodegeneration

To apply, candidates should email [maragkakise@nih.gov](mailto:maragkakise@nih.gov) with:

1. A cover letter describing research interests and how they align with the lab's work
2. A CV
3. Contact information for 2–3 references

## Graduate Students

Graduate students at NIH-affiliated programs (e.g., GPP, GPBB) are welcome to rotate.
Interested students should reach out to discuss opportunities.

## Visiting Scientists and Interns

The lab occasionally hosts visiting scientists and undergraduate interns. Interested candidates
should send a CV and a brief statement of interest to
[maragkakise2@nih.gov](mailto:maragkakise2@nih.gov).
