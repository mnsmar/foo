The Computational Genomics Unit is looking for motivated researchers to join our team
at the **National Institute on Aging (NIA), NIH** in Baltimore, MD.

We are an interdisciplinary group combining experimental and computational biology.
Ideal candidates have a passion for understanding how RNA and gene regulation drive aging,
and enthusiasm for developing and applying cutting-edge sequencing technologies and
machine learning tools.

## Postdoctoral Fellows

We have openings for postdoctoral fellows with expertise in one or more of:

- RNA biology, transcriptomics, or epitranscriptomics
- Nanopore / long-read sequencing
- Computational genomics or bioinformatics
- Machine learning applied to biological data
- Aging, senescence, or neurodegeneration

To apply, please email [maragkakise2@nih.gov](mailto:maragkakise2@nih.gov) with:

1. A cover letter describing your research interests and how they align with our work
2. Your CV
3. Contact information for 2–3 references

## Graduate Students

Graduate students at NIH-affiliated programs (e.g., GPP, GPBB) are welcome to rotate.
Please reach out to discuss opportunities.

## Visiting Scientists and Interns

We occasionally host visiting scientists and undergraduate interns. If you are interested,
please send your CV and a brief statement of interest to
[maragkakise2@nih.gov](mailto:maragkakise2@nih.gov).
