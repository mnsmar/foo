## Abstract

DNA hydroxymethylation (5hmC) is an epigenetic mark enriched in gene bodies of actively
transcribed genes. This study reveals that 5hmC in gene bodies restricts the magnitude of
transcriptional changes that occur during aging, identifying a novel epigenetic mechanism
that modulates gene expression homeostasis across the lifespan.
